# @webship-js/vikunja-steps

Cucumber step definitions for Vikunja, the open-source to-do and project management app, a pack for
[webship-js](https://github.com/webship/webship-js) (Playwright and Cucumber): logging in, a project and who may open
it, its tasks and one task, done or not, its labels, comments and assignees, a new task, and its views.

The steps, their documentation and CI come with the first release, `2.0.0`.
